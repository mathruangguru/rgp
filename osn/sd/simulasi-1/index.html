28. Jawaban: C
Gunakan metode pengurangan luas.
Luas ABCD $= 180$.
Luas $\Delta EBF = \frac{1}{2} \times EB \times BF = \frac{1}{2} \times (\frac{2}{3}p) \times (\frac{2}{3}l) = \frac{2}{9} \times 180 = 40$.
Luas $\Delta FCD = \frac{1}{2} \times FC \times CD = \frac{1}{2} \times (\frac{1}{3}l) \times p = \frac{1}{6} \times 180 = 30$.
Luas $\Delta DAE = \frac{1}{2} \times DA \times AE = \frac{1}{2} \times l \times (\frac{1}{3}p) = \frac{1}{6} \times 180 = 30$.
Luas Arsiran (DEF) $= 180 - (40 + 30 + 30) = 80$ cm$^2$.
