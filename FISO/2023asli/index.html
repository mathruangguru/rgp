Basic Theory: Addition of algebraic fractions.

Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Problem: $\frac{2}{x} + \frac{x}{x+1} + \frac{x-2}{x} = \frac{5}{4}$
  2. Combine the terms with denominator $x$: $$ \begin{aligned} \left(\frac{2}{x} + \frac{x-2}{x}\right) + \frac{x}{x+1} &= \frac{5}{4} \\ \frac{2 + (x-2)}{x} + \frac{x}{x+1} &= \frac{5}{4} \\ \frac{x}{x} + \frac{x}{x+1} &= \frac{5}{4} \\ 1 + \frac{x}{x+1} &= \frac{5}{4} \end{aligned} $$

Answer: C. $2\sqrt{3}$
Basic Theory: Trigonometry in Right-Angled Triangles & Properties of a Hexagon.

Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Angle Analysis:
    • $ABCDEF$ is a regular hexagon, so $\angle FAB = 120^\circ$.
    • $[KA] \perp [AB]$ means $\angle KAB = 90^\circ$.
    • This means $\angle FAK = \angle FAB - \angle KAB = 120^\circ - 90^\circ = 30^\circ$.
  2. Side Length: $AF$ is a side of the hexagon, so $AF = AB = 3$.
  3. Triangle AKF Analysis:
    • In a regular hexagon, the short diagonal (like $FD$) is perpendicular to the side it connects to (like $AF$). This means $\angle AFK = 90^\circ$.
    • We now have a right-angled triangle $AKF$ (right-angled at $F$).
  4. Trigonometric Calculation:
    • We know the angle $\angle FAK = 30^\circ$.
    • We know the adjacent side $AF = 3$.
    • We are looking for the hypotenuse $AK$.
    $$ \begin{aligned} \cos(\angle FAK) &= \frac{\text{Adjacent (AF)}}{\text{Hypotenuse (AK)}} \\ \cos(30^\circ) &= \frac{3}{AK} \\ \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} &= \frac{3}{AK} \\ AK \cdot \sqrt{3} &= 6 \\ AK &= \frac{6}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{6\sqrt{3}}{3} \\ AK &= 2\sqrt{3} \end{aligned} $$

Step-by-Step Solution:

    • P = $7.2 + 7.8 + 7.2 + 2.3 + 5.4 + 3.4 + 5.4 + 2.1$
