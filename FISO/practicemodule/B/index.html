Answer Key & Discussions

1.
Answer: B. 8
Basic Theory: System of Linear Equations.

Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Given: (1) $a + 2b = 7$, (2) $b + 2c = 8$, (3) $c + 2a = 9$.
  2. Add all three equations: $$ \begin{aligned} (a + 2b) + (b + 2c) + (c + 2a) &= 7 + 8 + 9 \\ 3a + 3b + 3c &= 24 \end{aligned} $$
  3. Factor out 3: $3(a + b + c) = 24$.
  4. Divide by 3: $a + b + c = 8$.
2.
Answer: A. 6
Basic Theory: Properties of Exponents.

Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. We are given $4^x = 9$.
  2. Rewrite 4 as $2^2$: $(2^2)^x = 9 \implies (2^x)^2 = 9$.
  3. Take the square root of both sides: $2^x = 3$. (We take the positive root as $2^x$ is always positive).
  4. The question asks for $2^{x+1}$.
  5. Using exponent rules: $2^{x+1} = 2^x \cdot 2^1$.
  6. Substitute the value from step 3: $(3) \cdot 2 = 6$.

3.
Answer: B. 120 cm²
Basic Theory: Pythagorean Theorem ($a^2 + b^2 = c^2$).

Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Let $L$ = Length, $W$ = Width, $D$ = Diagonal.
  2. Given: $D = 17$ cm, $W = 8$ cm.
  3. By Pythagoras: $L^2 + W^2 = D^2$.
  4. $L^2 + 8^2 = 17^2 \implies L^2 + 64 = 289$.
  5. $L^2 = 289 - 64 = 225 \implies L = \sqrt{225} = 15$ cm.
  6. (This is the 8-15-17 Pythagorean triple).
  7. Area = $L \times W = 15 \times 8 = 120$ cm².

  5. Combine the numerator: $\frac{8\sqrt{2}}{2\sqrt{2}}$.
  6. Cancel $\sqrt{2}$ and divide 8 by 2: $\frac{8}{2} = 4$.

Basic Theory: Rational Algebraic Equations.

Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. The equation is: $\frac{x}{x-2} - \frac{3}{x} = 1$.
  2. Find a common denominator, which is $x(x-2)$.
  3. Multiply every term by $x(x-2)$: $$ x(x) - 3(x-2) = 1 \cdot x(x-2) $$
  4. $x^2 - 3x + 6 = x^2 - 2x$.
  5. The $x^2$ terms cancel out.
  6. $-3x + 6 = -2x \implies 6 = -2x + 3x \implies 6 = x$.

Answer: B. $\frac{x-3}{x+3}$
Basic Theory: Factoring and Simplifying Algebraic Fractions.

Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Invert the second fraction and change to multiplication: $\frac{x^2 - 9}{x^2 + x - 6} \cdot \frac{x-2}{x+3}$.
  2. Factor all polynomials:
    • $x^2 - 9 = (x-3)(x+3)$
    • $x^2 + x - 6 = (x+3)(x-2)$
  3. Substitute the factors: $\frac{(x-3)(x+3)}{(x+3)(x-2)} \cdot \frac{x-2}{x+3}$.
  4. Cancel terms that appear in the numerator and denominator: $(x+3)$ and $(x-2)$.
  5. We are left with: $\frac{x-3}{x+3}$.

  6. $A = \frac{ABC}{BC} = \frac{60}{20} = 3$.
  7. $B = \frac{ABC}{AC} = \frac{60}{15} = 4$.
  8. $C = \frac{ABC}{AB} = \frac{60}{12} = 5$.

Answer: C. 42 cm
Basic Theory: Perimeter of Composite Shapes (Displacement).

Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. This problem is best solved using "Perimeter Displacement".
  2. Imagine the shape's "bounding box," which is a large 10 cm x 8 cm rectangle.
  3. The perimeter of this outer rectangle is $P = 2 \times (10 + 8) = 36$ cm.
  4. Now, look at the "notch" cut from the top. The 4 cm horizontal segment *on the top edge* is "removed" (Perimeter: -4 cm).
  5. But, a 4 cm horizontal segment is *added* back at the bottom of the cut (Perimeter: +4 cm). These two segments cancel each other out.
  6. The only *new* lines added to the perimeter (which the original 10x8 rectangle did not have) are the two vertical sides of the cut.
  7. The description states the cut is 3 cm deep. We add *both* the left 3 cm side and the right 3 cm side.
  8. Final Perimeter = (Perimeter of Bounding Box) + (New Sides) = $36 + 3 + 3 = 42$ cm.
