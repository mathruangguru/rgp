1.
Answer: C. 120
Fundamental Theory: Multi-step percentage calculation.

Steps:
  • Total Pages: 200
  • Monday: Reads 20% of 200. $0.20 \times 200 = 40$ pages.
  • Remaining Pages: $200 - 40 = 160$ pages.
  • Tuesday: Reads 25% of the *remaining* pages. $0.25 \times 160 = 40$ pages.
  • Pages left to read: $160 \text{ (after Monday)} - 40 \text{ (read Tuesday)} = 120$ pages.

Answer: B. 4
Fundamental Theory: System of Linear Equations in Three Variables (SLETV).

Steps:
  1. Sum all equations: $2(a + b + c) = 5 + 8 + 7 = 20$. So $a + b + c = 10$.
  2. Find the value of each variable:
    • $a = (a+b+c) - (b+c) = 10 - 8 = 2$.
    • $b = (a+b+c) - (a+c) = 10 - 7 = 3$.
    • $c = (a+b+c) - (a+b) = 10 - 5 = 5$.
  3. Calculate the final expression: $a - b + c = 2 - 3 + 5 = 4$.

8.
Answer: B. 6 cm²
Fundamental Theory: Area of a square and area of a triangle (Area Subtraction Method).

Steps:
  1. The easiest way to find the area of $\triangle AEF$ is by subtracting the area of the square $ABCD$ by the area of the three right-angled triangles at the corners ($ABE$, $ADF$, and $ECF$).
  2. Area of Square $ABCD = \text{side} \times \text{side} = 4 \times 4 = 16 \text{ cm}^2$.
  3. $E$ is the midpoint of $BC$, so $BE = EC = 4/2 = 2 \text{ cm}$.
  4. $F$ is the midpoint of $CD$, so $CF = FD = 4/2 = 2 \text{ cm}$.
  5. Calculate the area of the 3 triangles:
    • Area $\triangle ABE$ (right-angled at $B$): $\frac{1}{2} \times AB \times BE = \frac{1}{2} \times 4 \times 2 = 4 \text{ cm}^2$.
    • Area $\triangle ADF$ (right-angled at $D$): $\frac{1}{2} \times AD \times DF = \frac{1}{2} \times 4 \times 2 = 4 \text{ cm}^2$.
    • Area $\triangle ECF$ (right-angled at $C$): $\frac{1}{2} \times EC \times CF = \frac{1}{2} \times 2 \times 2 = 2 \text{ cm}^2$.
  6. Calculate the area of $\triangle AEF$: $$ \text{Area}(AEF) = \text{Area}(ABCD) - \text{Area}(ABE) - \text{Area}(ADF) - \text{Area}(ECF) $$ $$ \text{Area}(AEF) = 16 - 4 - 4 - 2 = 6 \text{ cm}^2 $$

Answer: 10
Fundamental Theory: Pythagorean Theorem or Area Subtraction Method.

Method 1: Area Subtraction
  1. Area of the large square $ABCD = 4 \times 4 = 16 \text{ cm}^2$.
  2. There are 4 identical right-angled triangles in each corner (e.g., $\triangle HAE$).
  3. Triangle side lengths: $AE = 1$ cm. So $HA = AD - HD = 4 - 1 = 3$ cm.
  4. Area of one triangle $\triangle HAE = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \times 1 \times 3 = 1.5 \text{ cm}^2$.
  5. Area of four triangles = $4 \times 1.5 = 6 \text{ cm}^2$.
  6. Area of square $EFGH$ = Area of $ABCD$ - Area of 4 triangles.
    $16 - 6 = 10 \text{ cm}^2$.
Method 2: Pythagorean Theorem
  1. The side of the square $EFGH$ (e.g., $HE$) is the hypotenuse of the triangle $\triangle HAE$.
  2. $HE^2 = HA^2 + AE^2 = 3^2 + 1^2 = 9 + 1 = 10$.
  3. The area of the square $EFGH$ is $HE \times HE = HE^2$.
  4. Area = $10 \text{ cm}^2$.
